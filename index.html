My Next Chapter: Beekeeping

A practical pathway into your next livelihood.

Beekeeping is well suited to late-career adults: it’s gentle, meaningful, land-friendly, and can support both household needs and additional income.

What you’ll gain

Practical skills

Understand equipment, hive placement, basic bee behaviour, and safe handling — no previous experience required.

Life structure & purpose

Replace “office routine” with a new rhythm aligned to nature, seasons, and meaningful work.

Income possibilities

Learn how honey and bee products can become a small but real contribution to your monthly income.

Program outline (example 6-session journey)

  • Session 1: Who will I be after work? Designing your next chapter.
  • Session 2: Introduction to bees, hives & tools.
  • Session 3: Setting up your first hive & attracting bees.
  • Session 4: Caring for your bees through the seasons.
  • Session 5: Harvesting & bottling honey simply and safely.
  • Session 6: Sharing and selling your honey — pricing, stories, and simple markets.

The program can be offered as a series of short sessions, a compact 1–2 day workshop, or a blended journey with follow-up support.

For organizations

A different kind of retirement support for your staff.

Instead of only preparing employees to exit your payroll, you can help them step into the next phase of life with dignity, skills, and a sense of direction.

Why employers choose this program

  • It complements, rather than competes with, existing pension fund training.
  • It supports mental health and adjustment during the last years of service.
  • It offers something concrete and memorable as a retirement benefit.
  • It can be aligned with your wellness and ESG strategies.
  • It shows genuine care for staff beyond their last payslip.

We can customise the delivery format and depth to suit your staff profile, time constraints, and budget.

About Ntlafatso

We help people grow into their next chapter with purpose.

Ntlafatso means “improvement” or “growth”. We exist to support people as they move from formal employment into a new season of meaningful work, contribution, and wellbeing.

Our work is grounded in three simple beliefs:

  • Retirement is not an ending. It is a transition into a new identity.
  • People need real skills. Not everyone wants to “just rest” — many want to build, grow, and contribute.
  • Support must fit our context. We design with the Basotho reality in mind: land, extended family, and evolving economic pressures.

Ntlafatso is led by Hapiloe Molapo, drawing together years of experience in employee benefits, healthcare financing, and beekeeping through The Honey Shop, to offer something both practical and deeply human.

“Retirement shouldn’t feel like falling off a cliff. It should feel like turning a page into a chapter you have helped to write — with purpose, rhythm, and skills that keep you alive inside.”

— Hapiloe, Founder of Ntlafatso
