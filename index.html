My Next Chapter: Beekeeping

A practical, gentle introduction to beekeeping as a way to build rhythm, connection to land and a small but meaningful income stream.

Beekeeping is more than honey. It’s an invitation to slow down, to listen to seasons, and to build something living that doesn’t demand 8–5 office energy. This program is designed for people who are moving toward retirement, early exit, or a portfolio life – and want a skill that aligns with their health, capacity and pace.

  • Understand what beekeeping realistically looks like in our context (time, space, investment, safety).
  • Learn the basics: equipment, hive types, siting, and working with bees respectfully.
  • Explore different ways honey and hive products can support your income without becoming another full-time job.
  • Think through how beekeeping could fit into your own next-chapter plan – not as a fantasy, but as a grounded option.

Beyond job titles

What is “My Next Chapter”?

A space for people who are more than their job descriptions and want a life that fits who they’ve become – not who they were at 25.

Many people reach mid- or late-career and realise: the pace, pressure and politics of formal employment no longer match their bodies, values or season of life. But leaving can feel like falling off a cliff – especially when so much identity has been tied to work.

My Next Chapter is about designing a life that still feels meaningful and useful, without recreating the same burnout in a different form. It holds three strands:

  • Inner work: identity, purpose, grief, and the privilege and responsibility of choosing a new pace.
  • Health & rhythm: how your next chapter can support your physical, emotional and mental wellbeing.
  • Practical skills & income: gentle, real skills (like beekeeping, food preservation, small-scale production) that can support a dignified living.

You don’t need another hustle. You need a life that fits you now.

“It’s not about starting over; it’s about walking forward with everything that has shaped you – at a pace that honours your energy.”

The inner work

Choosing this chapter

For many of us, being able to step out of formal employment by choice is a privilege. It’s also disorienting. My work is rooted in the belief that you deserve a life that fits your current capacity and truth – not the expectations that were placed on you years ago.

We won’t rush through the inner questions: Who am I without the title? What pace feels honest now? What kind of work feels like care, not punishment, to my body and nervous system?

From there, we translate reflection into simple experiments and skills. Small steps. No pressure to turn everything into a business. Just stewardship of the life you have.
