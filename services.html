Services

Support for individuals and organisations navigating their next chapter.

Every offering here is built to be gentle, honest and practical. No hustle pressure, no product sales. Just clear conversations, simple tools and skills that can travel with you into whatever comes next.

For Individuals For Organisations

For individuals

These offerings are for people who are leaving formal work, changing careers, or simply choosing a slower, more intentional way of living. We work online with clients around the world.

1. Next Chapter Advisory Session

A 60–90 minute conversation to explore where you are, what you long for, and what is realistically possible in this season.

  • Gentle exploration of your current life, energy, responsibilities and hopes.
  • Clarifying what “enough” looks like – financially, emotionally and practically.
  • Identifying 2–3 grounded next steps you can take in the next month.

2. Rhythm & Lifestyle Redesign

A short series of sessions to design daily and weekly rhythms that support your body, mind, hands and spirit.

  • Mapping your real constraints and non-negotiables.
  • Creating routines that prioritise rest, health and what matters most.
  • Building in space for skills, creativity, faith and relationships.

3. My Next Chapter: Beekeeping

A program for people who want to explore beekeeping as part of their next chapter — whether for meaning, gentle income, or both.

  • Realistic assessment of time, space, climate and safety in your context.
  • Foundations: hive types, basic equipment, siting, and working with bees respectfully.
  • Thinking through how beekeeping fits alongside your other responsibilities.

For organisations

We partner with employers who want to care well for staff in transition — especially those approaching retirement or stepping into new life seasons. These are not financial product sessions. They are human, practical conversations.

1. Pre-Retirement Conversations

Group or small-cohort sessions for employees within a defined number of years from retirement, or those taking early exit packages.

  • Exploring identity beyond the job title.
  • Thinking through health, routines and relationships after exit.
  • Understanding lifestyle implications of different choices.

2. Wellness & Pace Workshops

Gentle workshops for teams who are tired, stretched or navigating organisational change.

  • Slowing down enough to notice stress signals.
  • Practical tools for sustainable pacing and rest.
  • Inviting conversations around meaning, values and boundaries.

3. Skills & Micro-Experiences

When feasible, we host small experiences (in-person or hybrid) such as beekeeping introductions, food preservation demonstrations or reflective journaling sessions.
